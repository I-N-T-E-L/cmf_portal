CMF Evidence-Led Ownership Policy

Assess evidence for fix ownership first. OEM means the system manufacturer (for example Lenovo, HP, Dell, or Panasonic), not the end user. Consider all six risk metrics below; they support the decision but never determine or override the tag. Unknown metric evidence remains unknown, not a default low-risk score.

S | Severity | Weight 25 | 5=cannot power/boot, unrecoverable failure, permanent damage, data loss/corruption, certification failure; 4=BSOD, system hang, black screen, reboot/shutdown, major feature/I/O/network loss, severe performance degradation; 3=specific function/driver lost, application hang, intermittent malfunction, moderate performance degradation; 2=warning/event-log error, wrong LED, minor unexpected behavior with limited functional impact; 1=cosmetic issue, typo, wrong status/description, negligible functional impact.
O | Occurrence | Weight 15 | 5=always_100%; 4=frequently_50%-90%; 3=sporadic_10%-40%; 2=very_sporadic_<10%; 1=once. Compare categories with measured rates and denominators. Do not equate unit rates with cycle rates. If materially contradictory, explain the conflict and leave the score unknown rather than silently selecting a source.
D | Detection | Weight 20 | 5=spontaneous failure during ordinary use or idle without a specific feature-transition trigger; 4=daily core transition/workflow such as login/logout, ordinary wake/resume, S0ix, AC attach/detach, or another frequently used feature; 3=specific less-frequent customer workflow such as OOBE, AFT, S4/S5, restart, or optional feature use; 2=repeated stress testing, corner-case scenarios, or non-frequent operations; 1=special steps/settings/device, debug/lab-only setup, non-preloaded app, or unique manufacturing process. Ordinary resume is 4, not 5 merely because it is visible. Stress-only reproduction is 2, not 3 merely because the test is a named workflow.
R | Recovery | Weight 10 | 5=no acceptable workaround or recovery exists; 4=workaround significantly impacts usability or materially blocks validation; 3=recovery requires warm boot/reboot, BIOS change, reconnect, reflash, or repeated intervention; 2=simple reliable user action resolves the issue with minor inconvenience; 1=fully effective workaround with negligible usability or validation impact.
B | Business Impact | Weight 15 | 5=production/launch gate, executive escalation, financial impact, brand risk, competitive disadvantage; 4=customer milestone at risk, validation blocked, multiple escalations likely; 3=customer dissatisfaction, support burden, warranty/returns/service exposure possible; 2=limited business exposure or single-customer observation; 1=internal-only issue with no identified customer or business impact.
CX | Customer Experience | Weight 15 | 5=customer visible feature unusable, such as audio, camera, display, touch, or network unavailable; 4=significant visible feature degradation materially disrupts normal use; 3=intermittent visible degradation noticed by user but feature remains partially usable or recovers; 2=minor visible annoyance with no meaningful functional loss; 1=not visible to end user, debug-only symptom, status mismatch, or internal observation.

Severity, recovery, customer visibility, validation blockage, milestones, production and certification risks must influence the assessment and urgency, but do not prove which party owns the fix. Ownership needs source-backed causal findings or a controlled comparison identifying the responsible design/component. Existing tags, requests, assigned contacts, component names, attachment indicators, and reproduction breadth alone do not prove cause.

RVP reproduction and one-OEM scope guide investigation; neither alone proves Intel or OEM responsibility. Consider factual findings across descriptions, steps, comments and other supplied records. Do not claim to have examined an attachment if only its presence is supplied.

classification:
CMF_OK = evidence establishes an OEM design/implementation fault that the OEM is responsible for correcting.
CMF_REJECT = evidence establishes an Intel design/component/firmware/software fault that Intel is responsible for correcting. This is not a low-severity label.
CMF_REVIEW = evidence exists but is inconclusive, conflicting, or insufficient to establish a defensible fix owner; manual judgment is needed. AI/source retrieval failures also require review, not a claim that sighting evidence is absent.
CMF_INCOMPLETE = specific decision-critical facts are genuinely absent after checking every supplied source, including equivalent information in other fields. State what is missing, why it prevents a decision, and where it was searched. A blank dedicated field, unresolved cause, or nonessential missing log/recovery detail alone does not establish incompleteness.
