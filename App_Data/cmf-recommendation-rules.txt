  CMF_OK (Approve): Score >= 3.50 (or S>=3 with B>=3/RVP=Yes) | Min Conditions: S>=3 AND (B>=3 OR RVP=Yes) [Priority 1] OR Score>=3.50 [Priority 2] | Meaning: Meets primary severity/business/RVP criteria or secondary weighted score with confirmed gate. | Action: Approve CMF request and tag as CMF_OK.
  CMF_REVIEW (Review): Score >= 2.75 to < 3.50 (or S>=3 or B>=3 safeguard) | Min Conditions: S>=3 [Priority 3], B>=3 [Priority 4], or Score>=2.75 [Priority 5] | Meaning: Severity or confirmed gate safeguard met, or score in review band. Requires committee review. | Action: Route to CMF review meeting for manual disposition.
  CMF_REJECT (Reject): Score < 2.75 | Min Conditions: Otherwise [Priority 6] (RVP Yes alone does not qualify) | Meaning: Does not meet severity, confirmed gate, or weighted score threshold for CMF. | Action: Reject CMF request.
  CMF_INCOMPLETE (Incomplete): Score Condition-based (Any Score) | Min Conditions: Core metric evidence missing (Guardrail #3) | Meaning: One or more core categories cannot be scored from the available ticket evidence. | Action: Request missing reproduction, recovery, or impact details from the submitter before disposition.
  CMF_DUPLICATE (Duplicate): Score Condition-based (Any Score) | Min Conditions: Duplicate sighting or merge ID identified in ticket/comments | Meaning: Issue reports the same underlying failure already tracked under a primary sighting. | Action: Mark as cmf_duplicate and link to the primary sighting.
